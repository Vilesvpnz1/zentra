      Graphing Linear Equations

      Learn how to plot lines from an equation and identify slope and intercept from a graph.

      ▶ 04:12 / 11:30

      Key idea

      A linear equation can be written as y = mx + b, where m is the slope and b is the y-intercept. To graph a line, plot the intercept and use the slope to find a second point.

      Example

      Graph y = 2x − 4.

      1. The y-intercept is −4, so plot (0, −4).
      2. The slope is 2, so rise 2 and run 1 to get another point.
      3. Draw the line through both points.

      Check your understanding

      What is the slope of the line y = −3x + 7?

      Lesson notes

      Slope tells how steep a line is. Positive slope goes up left to right. Negative slope goes down.

      y-intercept is where the line crosses the y-axis.

      Vocabulary

      Slope
      Rate of change between two points
      Intercept
      Where the graph crosses an axis
      Linear
      A relationship with constant rate of change
