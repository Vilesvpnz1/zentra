Kobran doesn't really need your name or email to work. This page explains what little data might get touched when you browse around, and what we do with it (spoiler: not much).

What might get collected

  • Browser storage: Settings, tutorial flags, cloak preferences, and similar junk gets saved locally on your phone or laptop. That never leaves your device unless you sync browsers somehow.
  • Basic analytics: We may run lightweight stats scripts that see things like page hits, rough device type, or which section people click. Nothing super personal on purpose.
  • Server logs: If the site is hosted on a VPS or similar, normal logs exist. IP address, timestamp, browser string, thats the usual stuff hosts keep for security.

Why we'd use any of that

  • Keep features working and remember what you toggled on.
  • Figure out whats broken or slow so we can fix it.
  • Spot abuse, bot traffic, or someone hammering the server.

Third party embeds

When you open a game, proxy page, or VM, your leaving Kobran's bubble. Those sites have there own trackers, cookies, and policies. We don't control what Google, Hyperbeam, or some random .io game does with your data.

Cookies and local storage

Kobran itself might set cookies or use localStorage for prefs. Embedded content definitely might. You can wipe everything anytime in your browser settings if you want a clean slate.

Kids

Lots of people using unblocked hubs are students. If your under 13, ask a parent before using proxy tools or signing up for stuff on embedded sites. Laws vary by country and we're not lawyers.

Do we sell your info?

No. We don't sell personal data. Hosting providers and analytics might process technical logs because thats how the internet works, but we're not running a data broker side hustle.

Your choices

  • Clear site data in Chrome, Firefox, Safari, whatever you use.
  • Block analytics with an extension or DNS filter if you want.
  • Just don't use features you don't trust. Proxies are optional.

Updates to this policy

We might rewrite this page when features change. The date up top is when it last got touched. Checking back once in awhile is on you.
