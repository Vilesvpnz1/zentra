Key idea

A linear equation can be written as y = mx + b, where m is the slope and b is the y-intercept.

Check your understanding

What is the slope of the line y = −3x + 7?

Lesson notes

Slope tells how steep a line is. The y-intercept is where the graph crosses the y-axis.

Vocabulary

Slope
Rate of change between two points
Intercept
Where the graph crosses an axis
